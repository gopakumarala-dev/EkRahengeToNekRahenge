THE SHORT VERSION

HISTORY IS
MORE THAN A LIST OF DATES.

The history of Bharat is a long story of peoples, kingdoms, communities, knowledge systems, trade, art, architecture, conflict, resistance and cultural change.

Different periods brought different political powers and social conditions. Understanding those changes requires looking at both what happened and the evidence through which we know it.
