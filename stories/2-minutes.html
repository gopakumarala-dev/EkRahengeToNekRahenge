THE HISTORICAL SEQUENCE

HISTORY IS A
CONTINUOUS STORY.

The history of Bharat stretches across thousands of years. Political powers changed, kingdoms rose and fell, communities interacted, knowledge developed and cultural traditions continued to evolve.

A chronological view helps us understand these changes without treating individual events as isolated episodes.
