01   PRIMARY SOURCES

ORIGINAL EVIDENCE

Primary sources may include inscriptions, manuscripts, administrative records, coins, contemporary accounts and archaeological evidence.

Where possible, the original source should be identified before a historical claim is presented.

02   TEXTS & RECORDS

DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE

Chronicles, travel accounts, court records, literary works and other historical documents can provide important evidence.

Their date, authorship, purpose and historical context must also be considered.

03   ARCHAEOLOGY

MATERIAL EVIDENCE

Archaeological remains, inscriptions, architecture, coins and other material evidence can help establish historical information independently of later narratives.

04   SCHOLARSHIP

INTERPRETATIONS

Scholarly interpretations may differ. Where significant differences exist, they should be identified rather than hidden.
