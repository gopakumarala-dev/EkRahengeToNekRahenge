INVASIONS OF BHARAT

A chronological exploration of invasions, campaigns, conflicts and political upheavals that affected the civilisation of Bharat.

No blind belief. No selective memory. Follow the evidence.

01   CONTEXT

UNDERSTANDING INVASIONS

The history of Bharat includes periods of migration, warfare, invasion, conquest, resistance and political transformation.

This section will examine these events chronologically, distinguish between different types of conflict, and present the evidence used to understand them.

OUR APPROACH Every major claim should be connected to evidence, identified sources and historical context.
