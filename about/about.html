ABOUT THE PROJECT

WHY WE EXIST.

Ek Rahenge To Nek Rahenge is a journey through the history and civilisation of Bharat — examining the past through sources, traditions and evidence.

01 • WHY THIS PROJECT

HISTORY
MATTERS.

The past shapes how we understand the present. It influences our culture, institutions, memories and the way we understand ourselves.

But history should not be approached through slogans alone. It should be examined through evidence, context and a willingness to ask difficult questions.

OUR APPROACH

No blind belief.
No selective memory.
Follow the evidence.

03 • OUR PRINCIPLES

HOW WE
APPROACH HISTORY.

01

EVIDENCE FIRST

Historical claims should be connected to identifiable evidence wherever possible.

02

CONTEXT MATTERS

Sources must be understood in the historical, political and cultural context in which they were created.

03

QUESTION EVERYTHING

A source is not automatically a conclusion. We examine authorship, date, purpose and transmission.

04

ACKNOWLEDGE UNCERTAINTY

Where evidence is incomplete or interpretations differ, that uncertainty should be visible.

04 • WHAT WE ARE NOT

HISTORY IS
NOT PROPAGANDA.

×

NOT BLIND BELIEF

×

NOT SELECTIVE MEMORY

Difficult parts of history should not be erased simply because they are uncomfortable.

×

NOT THE FINAL WORD

Historical scholarship evolves as new evidence is discovered and interpretations change.

05 • OUR PURPOSE

REMEMBER.
UNDERSTAND.
LEARN.
UNITE.

Our past is not to create hatred. It is to create clarity. Our future is not to repeat history. It is to rise above it.
