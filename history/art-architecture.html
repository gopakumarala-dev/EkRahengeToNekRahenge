BHARAT HERITAGE
ART • ARCHITECTURE • CULTURE

A CIVILISATION
OF CREATION

The Indian subcontinent contains a vast record of architecture, sculpture, painting, decorative arts, crafts and performing traditions developed across different regions and periods.

These traditions can be studied through surviving monuments, archaeological remains, inscriptions, manuscripts, artistic objects and historical records.

Architecture across Bharat developed in diverse regional and historical traditions. Temples, monasteries, forts, palaces, stepwells, gateways and civic structures provide important material evidence for studying these traditions.
