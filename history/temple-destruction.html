A HISTORICAL QUESTION

WHAT HAPPENED
TO THE TEMPLES?

Temple destruction is a subject that requires careful examination of individual events rather than a single general explanation. Historical records can describe different circumstances, motives and consequences.

Each claim should therefore be examined against the available evidence and the historical context of the period concerned.

QUESTION 01

WAS THE TEMPLE
DESTROYED?

Establishing whether a structure was destroyed requires evidence relating to the site, its history, inscriptions, archaeology and contemporary accounts.

QUESTION 02

WHEN DID
IT HAPPEN?

Dates should be established from the strongest available evidence and should distinguish between contemporary testimony and later traditions.

QUESTION 03

WHAT WAS
THE CONTEXT?

Political conflict, warfare, religious policy, economic interests and other historical circumstances may need to be examined for each individual case.

QUESTION 04

WHAT HAPPENED
AFTERWARDS?

Reconstruction, restoration, reuse, relocation or continued worship can form an important part of the historical record.
