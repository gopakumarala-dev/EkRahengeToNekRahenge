A HISTORY OF RESISTANCE

RESISTANCE
WAS NOT ONE STORY

Resistance took many forms across different periods of Indian history. It could involve military campaigns, political alliances, local uprisings, diplomatic action or organised popular movements.

Each episode needs to be examined in its own historical context, using contemporary records and other available evidence.

QUESTION 01

WHO
RESISTED?

Historical resistance could involve rulers, soldiers, local chiefs, communities, religious institutions and ordinary people.

QUESTION 02

WHAT WERE
THEY RESISTING?

The reasons varied from political control and territorial expansion to taxation, military occupation and challenges to established authority.

QUESTION 03

HOW DID
THEY RESIST?

Resistance could involve battlefield confrontation, fortification, alliances, diplomacy, guerrilla warfare, local mobilisation and other strategies.

QUESTION 04

WHAT WAS
THE OUTCOME?

Some resistance movements succeeded, some were defeated and others continued over generations. Their outcomes should be established from the historical record.

RESISTANCE LEAVES RECORDS.

Battles, inscriptions, monuments, chronicles, administrative records and oral traditions can preserve evidence of resistance. Each source must be examined according to its date, purpose and historical context.
