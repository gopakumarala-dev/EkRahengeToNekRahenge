02
BEFORE THE EMPIRES

THE STORY
BEGINS DEEPER

The history of Bharat does not begin with a king, a dynasty or a modern political boundary.

Long before the great kingdoms of recorded history, human communities were already living across the Indian subcontinent.

They hunted, gathered, created tools, painted rock shelters, cultivated land, domesticated animals, established settlements and developed increasingly complex ways of living.

The archaeological record gives us a window into these distant worlds — even where written records do not survive.
