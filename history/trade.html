THE INDIAN OCEAN
NETWORK

Maritime routes connected the western and eastern coasts of the subcontinent with trading centres around the Indian Ocean.

Ports served as points of exchange for goods, people, ideas and cultural influences. The seasonal monsoon winds were important to navigation across the Indian Ocean.
