BHARAT KINGDOMS
POLITICAL HISTORY

MANY KINGDOMS.
MANY HISTORIES.

The history of Bharat includes a succession of kingdoms, republics, empires and regional powers. Their boundaries, institutions and influence changed over time.

Understanding these political formations requires attention to chronology, geography, inscriptions, literary sources, coins and archaeological evidence.

HISTORICAL EVIDENCE

HOW DO WE
KNOW ABOUT THEM?

Political history is reconstructed from different categories of evidence. Each source has its own strengths, limitations and historical context.

INSCRIPTIONS COINS CHRONICLES LITERATURE ARCHAEOLOGY
QUESTION 01

WHERE WAS
THE KINGDOM?

Historical geography can be reconstructed using inscriptions, archaeological sites, literary references, coins and other evidence.

QUESTION 02

WHEN DID
IT EXIST?

Chronology should be established by comparing dated inscriptions, coins, literary evidence and other reliable historical records.

QUESTION 03

WHO
RULED?

Rulers and dynasties can often be identified through inscriptions, coins, genealogies, chronicles and other historical records.

QUESTION 04

HOW WAS
IT GOVERNED?

Administrative systems, taxation, law, military organisation and institutions varied across periods and regions.

KINGDOMS WERE MORE THAN BORDERS.

Political history also intersects with language, religion, art, architecture, education, trade and cultural exchange. These connections help place individual kingdoms within the wider civilisation.
