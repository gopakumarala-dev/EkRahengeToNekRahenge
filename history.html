01   HISTORY

UNDERSTANDING THE HISTORY OF BHARAT

The history of Bharat includes periods of civilisation, migration, warfare, invasion, resistance, political transformation, trade, scholarship and cultural development.

Our approach is to examine historical claims through sources, chronology and context rather than relying only on inherited narratives or assumptions.

OUR APPROACH Every major historical claim should be connected to evidence, identified sources and historical context.
